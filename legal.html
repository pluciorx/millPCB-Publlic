Formats and imports

millPCB writes and reads files with homemade exporters and importers. It does not license Autodesk, KiCad, or GRBL; it only emits or reads files in those formats. User-imported KiCad files remain the user’s license problem.

  • SVG — W3C SVG 1.1
  • DXF R2000 — Autodesk DXF reference
  • G-code — GRBL-style; NIST RS-274 and GRBL
  • .pcb.json — millPCB’s own project save format (JSON, RFC 8259)
  • KiCad import — .kicad_mod, .kicad_sym, and .zip packages. Format docs: KiCad file formats. ZIP reading uses JSZip (MIT, above).

Privacy and cookies

The editor stores your project and UI state in the browser (localStorage). That stays on your device and is not sent to millpcb.com.

Optional Google Analytics (measurement ID G-LBBZFT66R7) loads only after you click Accept, and only on the editor. After Accept, Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_*. Google LLC processes that data, which may leave the visitor’s country. If you Reject, those analytics cookies are not used and the editor still works. Choice is stored as millpcb-cookie-consent in localStorage. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Change cookies — clears the stored choice so you can Accept or Reject again.
