millPCB — Easy PCB Design Tool for CNC Routers and Laser Cutters

millPCB is a free, easy-to-use web tool that lets hobbyists and makers design simple printed circuit boards (PCBs) directly in the browser and export them as SVG or DXF files ready to cut on a laser cutter or mill on a CNC router. No installation, no account, and no professional EDA experience required — just draw your board, run a quick design check, and download a file for your machine.
